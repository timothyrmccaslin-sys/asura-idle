## How they are made
`tools/music.py` synthesises every sound from scratch with numpy/scipy:
- **Drums:** sine-sweep kick, filtered-noise snare, hats and rim, plus a synthesised taiko.
- **Bass:** a sine with harmonics.
- **Keys:** an FM electric piano.
- **Koto:** a Karplus-Strong plucked string.
- **Shakuhachi:** a breathy sine with a meri slide and vibrato.
- **Texture:** tape hiss and vinyl crackle, made procedurally.

The script writes WAV files and they are encoded to MP3 with ffmpeg/LAME.

To regenerate:

```
python3 tools/music.py /tmp/music
for n in ink-road willow-rain oni-drums; do ffmpeg -i /tmp/music/$n.wav -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 96k assets/music/$n.mp3; done
cp /tmp/music/loops.json assets/music/
```
